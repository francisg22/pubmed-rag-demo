## Anaesthesia

A medicine used to stop you feeling pain during a procedure. It can be *local*
(numbs one small area while you stay awake), *regional* (numbs a larger part of the
body), or *general* (makes you fully unconscious for the operation).

## Physiotherapy

Treatment that uses guided exercises, movement, and hands-on techniques to help you
recover strength and movement after an injury, illness, or operation.

## Follow-up appointment

A later visit with your care team to check how you are healing, review any test
results, and answer questions after a treatment or procedure.
