The 2025 American College of Cardiology/American Heart Association guideline recommends an office blood pressure goal of <130/80 mm Hg for adults with confirmed hypertension, with encouragement to further reduce systolic blood pressure to <120 mm Hg [PMID 40874629]. Similarly, general recommendations suggest treating patients with uncomplicated hypertension to a target BP of <140/90 mmHg, or lower if tolerated, particularly in selected high cardiovascular risk populations [PMID 27456450].
